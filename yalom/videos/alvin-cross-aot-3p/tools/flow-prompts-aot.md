# Flow (Nano Banana) prompts — "Alvin Cross" anime re-make, Attack on Titan style

36 shots, 16:9. Every prompt is ONE line (Flow chat sends on Enter). Character names appear only as "inspired by …"; the visual anchors are repeated verbatim in every prompt so Flow never needs the name.

Expression rule for all prompts: calm, restrained, natural faces and body language. No theatrical poses, no open-mouth shouting, no tears streaming, no dramatic hand gestures. People stand and sit the way real people do.

Text rule: no text, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks anywhere, with two exceptions that are spelled out where they apply: the embroidered "Alvin Cross" on the white coat, and "MOLLY" on the headstone.
