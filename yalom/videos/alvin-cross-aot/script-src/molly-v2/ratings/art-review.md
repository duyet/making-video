- **b05:** "Alvin bay đến" over N07 (a car on a dirt road) is book-true (fly, then drive the dirt road). Optional: a 1-second insert at the cue of a plane window over clouds before N07.

A smaller group is glossier and cleaner, close to TV anime (R86, R05, N06, R46, R91, M02). These stand out when cut next to the inked shots. Draw new images to the inked baseline.
