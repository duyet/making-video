## Expression briefs (flagged images)

**R09 (a02, aged Yalom at the funeral, "khiến ông phát điên").** Fond exasperation remembered through grief. Brows: inner corners slightly raised, outer corners relaxed, no knit. Eyes: softly open and looking down and to the side at nothing (a memory), with a little crinkle at the outer corners. Mouth: a small *symmetric*, lips-closed smile that turns rueful, one breath away from a sigh, with no lifted corner. Jaw loose. Head tilted slightly down and to one side. No heavy upper lid: that is what makes the current version read smug.

**R11 (a03, at the gate).** Alvin: open and hopeful as he calls Yalom's name. Brows lifted and relaxed, eyes bright and fixed on Yalom, mouth half-open mid-word ("Dr. Yalom?") with the beginning of a smile, hand raised loosely rather than stiffly. Yalom: turning his head back over his shoulder, brows lifted in mild surprise, lips parted, no frown. Monica: a soft, warm smile, looking at Alvin and not at the lens.

**R12 (a03, Alvin close-up with the hat).** Earnest, warm, a little nervous. Brows level to slightly raised (no lowered outer brows). Eyes fully open, gentle, looking at Yalom just off camera rather than at the lens. Mouth: a modest, *even* smile, lips closed or slightly parted, with both corners lifted equally. Jaw relaxed. Head inclined forward in a small respectful nod, hat held against his chest. Avoid heavy lids and any one-sided corner lift.

**R13 (a04, handshake).** Alvin: grateful. Inner brows slightly up, eyes warm and creased at the corners, an open smile with a hint of teeth, leaning in slightly. Monica: smiling at *Alvin* (head turned toward him), eyes normal-sized with crinkles, mouth softly open. Yalom: polite and a little uncertain, brows lifted rather than knitted, a courteous closed smile, head slightly tilted.

**N06 / S15 (b03, "Jason, em trai anh, vừa qua đời").** Fresh grief, held in. Brows: inner corners drawn *up* and together (the grief shape), not pressed down. Eyes: lowered, lids heavy, a little wet, no glare. Mouth: lips pressed lightly together, corners down, chin faintly puckered. Jaw: not clenched. Head bowed a few degrees, shoulders sunk. (S15 still needs drawing. For b12 the current N06 is acceptable.)

**R46 (b03, "Jason").** A remembered face, worn rather than hostile. Brows: relaxed, slightly raised at the inner corners. Eyes: tired, looking off to the side into the distance, lids half-lowered, no hard focus on anything. Mouth: neutral to faintly downturned, lips slightly parted. Jaw slack. If the defiance from p.79 is wanted, keep it in the posture (chin a little up), not in a brow scowl. Soft, faded memory light.

**M01 (b04, the engagement dinner).** Suppressed shock, not open hostility (p.76: "nobody said anything"). Father: not centred and not facing the lens. Eyes on his plate or turned toward his wife, lips pressed thin, brows slightly raised in stiff disapproval. Mother: keep her worried look (inner brows up). Fiancée: uneasy and defiant, chin up, eyes sliding sideways toward Jason, mouth set but no snarl. Jason: defensive, a half-turn toward her, brows drawn but eyes hurt rather than glaring. Alvin: uncomfortable, eyes down, jaw tight, glancing at Jason. At most one person in the frame frowns hard.

**S12 (b04, Jason leaving at night).** Furtive and guilty. Brows: slightly raised and tense, not lowered. Eyes: a quick glance back toward the stairs (where the family sleeps), wide but not bulging. Mouth: lips pressed, faint tension at the corners. Body already turning out of the door. Fear of being heard, not anger.

**N08 (b06, the brothers at the fireplace).** Alvin: tentative and awkward. Inner brows raised, eyes searching Jason's face, mouth half-open as if about to speak and failing, hand lifted halfway and hesitant rather than lecturing, back straight against the chair (the distance is the point). Jason: keep his flat, worn sideways look.

**R05 (b07, Alvin at the film board).** Haunted stillness, not a jump-scare. Brows: inner corners slightly raised, outer corners relaxed. Eyes: normal aperture with the iris touching the upper lid (no white ring), unfocused, fixed on the film. Mouth: closed, slack, corners slightly down. Jaw loose. Head a little forward, shoulders still.

**R85 (b09, young Yalom listening).** Compassionate attention. Brows: inner corners gently raised (concern), no knit. Eyes: soft, steady on Alvin, slight crinkle. Mouth: closed, relaxed, corners neutral to faintly up. Head tilted a few degrees toward Alvin, pen resting rather than poised.

**F26 (b09a, the parents' photo).** A real, warm snapshot. Both parents smiling naturally, the father with eye crinkles and an easy, slightly lopsided smile, shoulders angled toward his wife, eyes not dead centre. Faded colour, film grain, a white border, slight blur (CINEMATIC "Natural").

**R42 (b12, the woman leaving).** Alvin in the doorway: hurt and resigned. Brows: inner corners up. Eyes: following her, then dropping, no hard stare toward the lens. Mouth: closed, corners slightly down, lips pressed in embarrassment. Body half hidden by the door because of shame, not lurking. Lighting from the porch lamp should be soft, not horror under-lighting.

**R90 / S09 (b14 3p / 1p, the front door).** Book note (p.101): the door was ajar with a "Cứ vào" note and Alvin stayed in his chair facing the window, so the better fix is a door-only shot (ajar door, note taped on it, Yalom's hand). If Alvin stays in the shot, the cue is reluctant embarrassment: brows slightly raised and drawn together, eyes lowered and avoiding Yalom, mouth in a tight, apologetic line, shoulders hunched, the hand on the door hesitant, half blocking the door, not gesturing him in. No set jaw, no stare.

**F18 (b15, Yalom trying to reach Alvin).** Yalom: gentle and careful. Brows: inner corners raised, no furrow. Eyes: soft, looking at Alvin. Mouth: slightly open, speaking quietly. Body leaning in a little, palm open and low. Alvin: keep the crossed arms and shut eyes (shame). Better still per the book: Alvin in his chair facing the window, Yalom seated behind him.

**R06 (c03 / c05, the café two-shot).** c03: an easy reunion between two old men. Yalom: brows lifted in curiosity, eyes bright, a faint open smile, leaning in. Alvin: a relaxed, warm smile, eyes crinkled, mid-story, hand loose on his cup, no fixed stare. For c05 use a separate Yalom close-up (or a variant): humbled and moved, inner brows lifted, eyes lowered and a little glistening, lips parted on a soft exhale, a slow small shake of the head.

**R07 (c04, Alvin reminding Yalom about Molly).** Book p.105: Alvin is taken aback that Yalom doesn't remember, then grateful. Brows: raised in gentle surprise. Eyes: fully open, warm, on Yalom, a little crinkle. Mouth: an open, even smile, starting to laugh softly, never one-sided. Chin level or slightly down. Head tilted toward Yalom, a hand half-raised as if saying "you don't remember?"

**M06 (c04, Molly at Alvin's door).** Brisk but kind, unfazed by the mess. Molly: brows relaxed, eyes taking in the room with a practical glint, a small confident half-smile (both corners), one hand on her hip and the other already reaching for a stack. Alvin: keep his sheepish, brows-up look.

**R92 (c06, Alvin and Monica with the photo).** Tender contentment. Alvin: brows relaxed and slightly lifted, no low heavy brow, eyes soft and crinkled looking at the photo, an easy smile with lips slightly parted. Monica: eyes normal-sized, crinkled, smiling down at the photo. The hand on her shoulder should be relaxed, not posed (CINEMATIC "Natural").
