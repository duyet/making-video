# Shot-rhythm additions — X01–X13

Same house format as `tools/flow-msgs/B1.txt` and `script-src/molly-plan/new-shots.md`: one line per prompt, "inspired by" names only, full identity anchors repeated in every prompt, even readable lighting, calm restrained expressions, no text / captions / watermark (the only legible text allowed is the embroidered "Alvin Cross" on the white coat where stated). Period: 1950s–60s rural flashbacks, 1982 Stanford and Palo Alto, present-day cemetery kept in the same timeless 1980s–90s look as F23/F13.

Thirteen shots: 4 toàn, 1 trung, 5 cận, 3 inserts. Ten are used in both scripts. X01 supersedes the optional N20 (same beat, dawn instead of midday, so skip N20 if X01 is generated).
