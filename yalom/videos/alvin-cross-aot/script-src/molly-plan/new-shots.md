# "Thank You, Molly" — new shots (N01–N21)

Same conventions as `tools/flow-prompts-aot.md`: one line per prompt, "inspired by" names only, full visual anchors repeated in every prompt, calm restrained expressions. Two changes for this set: every prompt asks for **even, readable lighting** (the existing set drifted dark), and the only legible text allowed is the embroidered "Alvin Cross" on the white coat where stated.

Period notes baked into the prompts: 1982 Stanford (wall-mounted X-ray lightboxes, beige rotary phones, film jackets), 1980s California bungalow, 1982 North Carolina (pine woods, red-clay dirt road, a plain clapboard house with a fieldstone fireplace), present day for the funeral/café/family shots kept in the same 1980s–90s look as F23/F13 so nothing in frame dates it.

## Anchors used below

- **YALOM** — Dr. Yalom: man about 50, inspired by Zeke Yeager, short tousled dark-blond hair, full trimmed blond beard, round wire-rim glasses, calm intellectual face, kind tired eyes, medium build; grey wool overcoat over a brown knit cardigan, dark trousers.
- **YALOM (indoors)** — same, brown knit cardigan over a shirt, no overcoat.
- **ALVIN (work 1982)** — Alvin Cross: man mid-30s, inspired by Erwin Smith, tall, blond hair in a neat side part with silver-grey temples, strong angular jaw, heavy brows, blue eyes, commanding calm; white doctor coat with "Alvin Cross" embroidered in dark blue on the left chest, pale blue shirt, dark tie.
- **ALVIN (home 1982)** — same man, guarded calm; olive knit sweater over a collared shirt, grey trousers.
- **ALVIN (present, ~40)** — Alvin Cross: man about 40, inspired by Erwin Smith, tall, blond hair in a neat side part with silver-grey temples, strong angular jaw, heavy brows, blue eyes, quiet warmth; charcoal suit, white shirt, black tie, dark overcoat, a cream Panama hat with a black band.
- **MONICA** — Monica: woman early 30s, inspired by Historia Reiss, small and slender, long pale blonde hair, large blue eyes, gentle quiet face, soft smile.
- **MOLLY** — Molly: woman in her 50s, Hange-like practical energy, brown hair in a loose tied-back ponytail, small round glasses, warm no-nonsense face, sturdy build; beige apron over a rolled-sleeve checked shirt, work trousers.
- **JASON** — Jason Cross: man about 30, inspired by Eren Yeager, dark brown shoulder-length hair, intense green eyes, defiant tired stare, lean wiry build, light stubble; faded denim jacket over a grey work shirt, dusty work jeans, boots.
